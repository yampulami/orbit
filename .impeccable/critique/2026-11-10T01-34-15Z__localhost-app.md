## Design Specificity Verdict
Look is Orbit's (flat, one persimmon accent, orb). Structure is not: eight equal tabs read like a collection of student-app features. The unique idea (find the hour you are free together) is the least visible.
Deterministic scan: 0 findings in web/src/pages and web/src/components. Overlay not run.

## Priority issues
- [P1] One prompt told four times (Feed headline + row, Chats header, Plans header, nav dot, bell). Fix: one Needs-you strip; elsewhere a count. /impeccable distill
- [P1] Schedule spread across four tabs (Feed timeline/Calendar, Plans month/list/day/week, Free time, Campus events); Find a time has four entry points. Fix: merge Free time into Plans (Day/Week/Month + Find a time as primary). /impeccable shape then layout
- [P1] Navigation costs recall: icon-only rail, second icon-only rail in Campus, two avatars, Roommates equal weight for commuters. Fix: labelled rail with 5 destinations (Home, Chats, Plans, Campus with Events|Clubs|Map tabs, You); utilities in avatar menu; Roommates only for residents. /impeccable clarify then layout
- [P2] Feed buries the feed: five blocks before the first post; events duplicate the right Calendar; default Everything shows resident-only posts to commuters. Fix: one Now card then posts; filter default from living. /impeccable layout
- [P2] Feel: floating orb covers content on every page; two RSVP vocabularies; blank paint on tab change. Fix: dock orb; one vocabulary; prefetch + skeletons. /impeccable polish, animate
